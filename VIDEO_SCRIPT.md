# CHW Companion — 3-Minute Video Script
## Archive — Gemma 4 Good Hackathon (closed 2026-05-18)

Kept as the demo script that shipped with the prototype. There is no live
hackathon deadline and no judge to submit this to.

**Total target: 2:45 — leaves 15s buffer under the 3-minute cap**
**Tone: calm, urgent, human. Not a tech pitch. A story about a decision.**

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES (read before filming)

- **Format:** Screen recording of the app + voiceover. No talking head required.
- **Screen recording:** Use OBS or Windows Game Bar (Win+G). Record at 1080p.
- **Browser:** Open `app/index.html` in Chrome, zoomed to 125%, mobile-first view (DevTools → iPhone 14 Pro). This shows the app as a CHW would see it on their phone.
- **Offline demo:** Turn on airplane mode visibly (show the Windows taskbar losing WiFi) before the cache scenario demos. Turn it back on for live inference.
- **Music:** Soft ambient (Pixabay "Hope" or similar). Quiet under narration, not over it.
- **Captions:** Add auto-captions via YouTube. Review for accuracy on medical terms.

## SEGMENT 4 — HOW IT WORKS (1:50 – 2:20)

**[SCREEN: Switch to ARCHITECTURE.md rendered as simple diagram — or draw as a quick slide.]**

> *NARRATION:*
> "The architecture is deliberately simple.
>
> Gemma 4 E4B powers two things:
> A 20-scenario cache — Gemma 4 outputs, curated for clinical accuracy,
> bundled inside the app as static JSON. Works on any device. No network needed.
>
> And a live two-phase inference endpoint —
> fast primary verdict, then streaming clinical detail.
> Both served from an open GPU tier.
>
> The phone is a thin client.
> Gemma 4 is the brain."

**[SCREEN: Show the code repo briefly — server.py open, MODEL_ID line visible. Then close.]**

> *NARRATION:*
> "Apache 2.0. Every line open.
